Who We Are

India's Most Integrated NPA Resolution Platform

SlashNPA is a specialized resolution company that combines legal expertise, physical asset management, and structured settlement facilitation into a single, accountable execution framework.

Unlike traditional service providers who operate in silos, we deliver end-to-end recovery solutions. Our model eliminates coordination gaps, accelerates timelines, and maximizes recovery values through unified command and control.

Founded by professionals with deep domain expertise in banking, law, and asset management, SlashNPA is built on the belief that NPA resolution requires an integrated approach — not fragmented vendor management.

Our Mission

"To become India's most trusted NPA resolution partner by delivering consistent, compliant, and value-maximizing outcomes through an integrated execution model."

Our Vision

"A financial ecosystem where stressed assets are resolved swiftly, ethically, and with full value preservation — benefiting lenders, borrowers, and the economy."
