Overview

Protecting Asset Value at Every Stage

AssetShield is SlashNPA's physical asset management vertical. We handle the critical ground-level operations that determine whether a secured asset retains its value through the recovery process — or deteriorates into a fraction of its worth.

From the moment a possession order is executed, our teams ensure continuous security presence, systematic inventory documentation, property maintenance, and eventually, technology-driven e-auction processes that maximize realization values.

The Asset Value Problem

Without proper custody and management, possessed assets lose 20-40% of their value within months. Encroachment, vandalism, weather damage, and legal complications erode recovery potential daily. AssetShield eliminates this risk.
