Our Expertise

Legal Forums & Proceedings

⚖️ SARFAESI Act Enforcement

The Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act is the primary tool for secured creditors. Our team manages the entire SARFAESI lifecycle:

  • ✓ Section 13(2) Demand Notice drafting & issuance
  • ✓ Section 13(4) Possession Notice preparation
  • ✓ Symbolic & physical possession coordination
  • ✓ Section 14 applications before CMM/DM
  • ✓ Asset valuation & e-auction facilitation
  • ✓ Objection handling & reply management
  • ✓ DRT appeal defense (Section 17)
  • ✓ Sale certificate & title transfer

🏛️ Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT)

For cases requiring adjudication through DRT proceedings, our specialized tribunal practice covers:

  • ✓ Original Application (OA) filing & prosecution
  • ✓ Recovery Certificate execution
  • ✓ Interim applications & injunctions
  • ✓ DRAT appeals management
  • ✓ Attachment & sale proceedings
  • ✓ Defendant tracing & service

📋 Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC)

For resolution of corporate and personal insolvencies under IBC framework:

  • ✓ Section 7 application filing (Financial Creditor)
  • ✓ Committee of Creditors (CoC) representation
  • ✓ Resolution plan evaluation support
  • ✓ CIRP monitoring & compliance
  • ✓ Liquidation proceedings support
  • ✓ Personal guarantor insolvency
