Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Non-Performing Asset (NPA)? +
An asset, including a leased asset, becomes non-performing when it ceases to generate income for the bank. Typically, it refers to loans where interest or installments stay overdue for 90 days.
Can an NPA account be settled without going to court? +
Yes, through One-Time Settlement (OTS) and structured negotiations. SlashNPA specializes in facilitating these resolutions ethically and swiftly.
How does LegalForce help after a SARFAESI notice? +
We manage the entire lifecycle—from drafting replies to Section 13(2) notices to defending Section 17 appeals in DRT and executing physical possession.
