The Challenge

The Execution Gap

Banks and financial institutions lose billions annually — not due to lack of legal provisions, but because of fragmented execution across recovery processes.

Why NPAs Persist

The Indian banking sector faces a systemic challenge: while legal frameworks like SARFAESI, DRT, and IBC exist, the real bottleneck lies in execution. Multiple vendors, inconsistent follow-ups, lack of coordination between legal and physical possession teams, and absence of a unified strategy result in delayed recoveries and eroded asset values.

SlashNPA bridges this gap with an integrated execution model that treats legal, physical, and settlement processes as one unified recovery lifecycle.

Industry Pain Points

  • ✕ Delayed possession & security lapses
  • ✕ Legal follow-up inconsistencies
  • ✕ Asset value deterioration
  • ✕ No unified MIS or dashboards
