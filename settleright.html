Overview

Beyond Traditional Recovery

SettleRight is SlashNPA's structured One-Time Settlement (OTS) facilitation vertical. We recognize that not every NPA requires aggressive legal enforcement — many cases achieve better outcomes through professionally managed settlement negotiations.

Our approach combines deep borrower profiling, realistic capacity assessment, institutional policy alignment, and structured negotiation frameworks to arrive at settlements that maximize recovery while ensuring borrower compliance.

The result: faster resolution, lower costs, and higher net recovery compared to prolonged litigation.

Why OTS Works

  • 01 Faster closure — months vs. years in litigation
  • 02 Lower cost of recovery per account
  • 03 Higher net realization (no auction discounts)
  • 04 Preserves borrower relationship for future business
The Process

4-Stage Resolution Framework

A systematic, proven approach to structured settlement that ensures optimal outcomes at every step.

1

Borrower Profiling & Assessment

Deep analysis of borrower's current financial position, asset base, income sources, liabilities, and realistic repayment capacity. We separate willful defaulters from genuine distress cases.

2

Capacity & Policy Alignment

Mapping borrower's true payment capacity against the institution's OTS policy framework. We identify the settlement zone where both parties' interests converge.

3

Structured Negotiation

Professional, multi-round negotiations with clear documentation at each stage. We manage expectations, handle counter-proposals, and drive towards mutually acceptable terms.

4

Documentation & Closure

Complete settlement documentation, payment schedule management, NOC issuance coordination, and account closure verification — ensuring zero loose ends.
